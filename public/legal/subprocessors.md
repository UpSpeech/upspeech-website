# Subprocessors

**Last Updated:** October 15, 2026

These companies process personal data for UpSpeech, on our instructions and under a contract with us. Under the GDPR they are our sub-processors. Our [Privacy Policy](/privacy/) explains what we collect and why.

We update this page before we add or replace a provider.

Where a provider processes data outside the European Economic Area, the transfer relies on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

## Hosting and storage

### Railway

- **What it does:** Hosts our servers and database
- **Data:** All platform data
- **Where:** European Union (Netherlands)

### Google Cloud Storage

- **What it does:** Stores audio and video recordings, and organisation logos
- **Data:** Recordings, which can contain health information
- **Where:** European Union

## AI

### ElevenLabs

- **What it does:** Turns uploaded recordings into text, and turns speech into text and text into speech in live practice conversations
- **Data:** Recordings, live audio, transcripts and report text
- **Where:** United States

### Groq

- **What it does:** Turns the audio from practice scenarios into text
- **Data:** Audio from practice scenarios
- **Where:** United States

### Google Gemini

- **What it does:** Language models that write report drafts, answer in the in-app chats, run practice conversations and the intake interview
- **Data:** Transcripts, the context a therapist writes for a report, chat messages, and the patient's name in practice conversations and the intake interview
- **Where:** United States and other countries where Google operates

### Langfuse

- **What it does:** Keeps a record of each AI request and response, to check quality and fix errors
- **Data:** AI requests and responses, including transcripts and chat messages
- **Where:** European Union (Ireland)

## Real-time media

### Cloudflare

- **What it does:** Relays the live audio and video of practice conversations when a direct connection is not possible
- **Data:** Live audio and video, passed through and not stored
- **Where:** The Cloudflare location nearest to you

## Email and notifications

### Resend

- **What it does:** Sends transactional email, such as invitations and password resets
- **Data:** Names, email addresses and email content
- **Where:** United States

### Expo, with Apple and Google

- **What it does:** Delivers push notifications to the mobile apps, through Apple Push Notification service and Firebase Cloud Messaging
- **Data:** Device push tokens, and the title and text of each notification
- **Where:** United States

## Analytics and error tracking

### PostHog

- **What it does:** Product analytics
- **Data:** Usage events and user identifiers
- **Where:** European Union

### Sentry

- **What it does:** Error tracking
- **Data:** Error reports, which can include an IP address
- **Where:** European Union (Germany)

## Website

### Netlify

- **What it does:** Hosts this website and receives the early-access form
- **Data:** Form submissions (name, email and role), and visitor logs
- **Where:** United States
