## Caption

```text
Most children who stutter stop. Nobody can tell which.

About 5 in every 100 children go through a period of stuttering that lasts six
months or more. Most of them stop. Around 1 in 100 keep stuttering long term
(Stuttering Foundation).

Both numbers are true, and together they are the reason families wait. From the
outside there is no way to tell which group a child is in.

Asking a speech and language therapist early is not the same as something being
wrong. It is how you find out.

Save this for the next time somebody tells a parent to wait and see.

#Stuttering #StutterPositive #PeopleWhoStutter #Stammering #SpeechTherapy #SLP
```

## Notes

**The design changed from the first draft, and so did the copy on the image.**
The first version put the myth as a single headline. The version that shipped
puts three things people actually say to parents into speech bubbles, then
answers all three at once underneath. It is the same brief and the same evidence.
It is a better post because the reader recognizes the sentences before they read
the correction.

The education pillar carries nothing to sell, so there is no product mention and
no link-in-bio prompt.

The recovery figure is the one `proof-library.md` flags as easy to misread. This
post exists to hold both halves together: the statistic, and the reason it should
not become a reason to wait.

**Portuguese version still to do.** The numbers and the three bubbles are baked
into the image, so PT needs its own export rather than a translated caption. The
bubbles will need shortening: Portuguese runs 15 to 25 percent longer and each
bubble is a single line by design.
